Our Story

Because ✶ good treats ✶
and great connections are
things we all Knead.

At Kneadforit., we believe that food does more than satisfy a craving—it brings people together, creates lasting memories, and builds community. Founded in 2022, our contemporary neighbourhood bakery was born out of a deep passion for baking and sharing delicious, thoughtfully crafted treats.

Our name is a humorous, playful nod to the idea that just as dough needs time and care to rise, we all need connection. By blending classic, comforting flavours with modern presentation, clean design, and inventive recipes, Kfi. encourages you to savour freshly baked goods and experience the simple joy of baking.

Our Mission

To unite people through accessible digital content, distinctive baking experiences, interactive classes, and fresh, locally made treats. We foster creativity, connection, and individuality in a warm, community-driven environment, making baking accessible and rewarding for all skill levels.

Our Vision

To become a beloved neighbourhood landmark and vibrant online hub that inspires people to bake, connect, and craft unforgettable memories through food. Combining high-quality bakes with a friendly, welcoming spirit, Kneadforit. serves as a destination for younger foodies, families, and baking lovers alike.
