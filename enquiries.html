✶ Frequently Asked Questions. ✶

Do you offer delivery, or is it pickup only? +
We offer both local pickup and delivery options depending on your location.
How should I store my bento cake or sourdough bread after pickup? +
Keep bento cakes refrigerated and sourdough bread wrapped at room temperature.
Can you accommodate dietary restrictions or food allergies? +
Please let us know in your enquiry form, and we will do our best to accommodate.
How far in advance do I need to order a custom bento cake? +
We recommend booking at least 3 to 5 days in advance.
Can I send reference photos for my custom cake design? +
Yes, you can upload or attach reference photos in the enquiry response.
Do I need to bring my own equipment or ingredients to a workshop? +
All ingredients and equipment are fully provided in our classes.
Can I book a private baking workshop for a group or team event? +
Yes, private group bookings are available upon request!
What is your cancellation or rescheduling policy for classes? +
Cancellations made 48 hours prior to class are eligible for full rescheduling.
