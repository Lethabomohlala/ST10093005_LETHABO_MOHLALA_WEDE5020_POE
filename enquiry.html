Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Go to the Enquiries/FAQ page and fill out the form to enquire to place an order. After choosing "Order Request" as your enquiry type, enter the product, flavour, quantity, desired date, and any other specifications in the messages box. To confirm your order, we will get in touch with you.

Do you offer custom cakes?

Yes. For birthdays, festivities, and other important events, KNEAD YOU provides personalised cakes. On the enquiry form, choose "Custom Order" or "Order Request," then provide your desired flavour, theme, design, size, and date.

How do I book a baking workshop?

There is a booking option on the front page. To secure a spot, fill out the form and choose "Class Booking" as your enquiry type. We will reply with more booking information and confirmation.

How far in advance should I place an order?

We advise placing your order request 3 to 5 days ahead of time. We advise getting in touch as soon as possible since specific requests, larger orders, or custom cakes could take longer to prepare.
